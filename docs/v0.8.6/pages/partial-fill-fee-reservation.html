Partial Fill Fee Reservation Remainder

Three separately floored fees met at a partial fill, and the one unit they could disagree by was released from the reservation ledger without reaching anyone. PartyA now receives it at the fill.

Fee reservation on a partial fill

sendQuote debits the open trading fee from PartyA’s allocation and records it as a reservation, on the basis described in Market Open Fee on Execution Price. When a LIMIT quote is partially filled, the filled part becomes a position and the remainder becomes a child quote with its own reservation. Each of the three fees is the floor of a product: 

parent  = floor(quantity        * price * tradingFee / 1e36)
filled  = floor(filledAmount    * price * tradingFee / 1e36)
child   = floor((quantity - filledAmount) * price * tradingFee / 1e36)

Flooring twice can drop more than flooring once, so parent can exceed filled + child by one unit.

The stranded unit

The open path released parent - child from the reservation ledger, which includes the extra unit, and credited the fee collector with the executed fee for the filled part. The reservation ledger and PartyA’s allocation had both been reduced by parent at sendQuote; after the fill the child held child, the collector held filled, and the extra unit was in neither. It stayed in the diamond with no owner. The exact conservation oracle in the repository fuzz runner reported the ledger one unit below the tokens held after every partial fill with non-divisible fee arithmetic.

Returning the remainder

At a partial open, openPosition computes parent - filled - child and, when it is positive, releases that amount from the reservation and credits it back to PartyA’s allocation as PLATFORM_FEE_IN. It then realises only filled against the reservation. The child quote keeps exactly the reservation its own formula produces, so a later open, cancellation, expiry or liquidation of the child unwinds precisely what was recorded for it. 

reservation after fill  = parent - filled - remainder = child
collector               += executed fee for the filled part
PartyA allocation       += remainder (0 or 1 unit)

What did not change

  1. Full fills have no child and no remainder; their fee handling is as before.
  2. The executed fee the collector receives is unchanged; it is still the reserved basis adjusted to the execution price for market quotes.
  3. The child quote’s locked values and status transitions are unchanged.
  4. A BalanceChangePartyA event with PLATFORM_FEE_IN can now appear in a partial-fill transaction for one unit. Indexers that reconcile fee reservations should expect it.
