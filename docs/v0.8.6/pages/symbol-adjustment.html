Symbol Corporate-Action Adjustment

A stock split or rebase changes the market's units, but open quotes still use the old ones. v0.8.6 freezes the symbol at the change, then either adjusts Muon's price or rewrites the stored quotes.

Why an adjustment is needed

A unit-changing event alters quantity and price together. After a 4:1 split, one old unit becomes four new units and the venue price falls to roughly one quarter. The position's economic value does not change.

Symmio's open quotes do not change when the venue does. A quote may still store a quantity of 1 and an opened price of 400 while the venue now reports 100. Using that raw venue price against the old quote would create false UPNL.

The adjustment flow keeps the quote and the price in the same units throughout the change. It covers stock splits, reverse splits, and rebases. It does not cover cash dividends, spin-offs, mergers, ticker migrations, or ordinary market moves.

The two routes

After the venue changes units and Core freezes the symbol, operations has two choices:

  1. Keep the quotes unchanged and convert Muon's venue price back into their old units. Trading can resume as soon as operations confirms that price basis. This is the active-factor route.
  2. Keep trading paused and rewrite every open quote into the venue's new units. Trading resumes only after the book is complete. This is the direct-restatement route.
Decision Active-factor route Direct-restatement route
What changes first Muon changes its price calculation. Core changes stored quotes.
When trading resumes After operations confirms the adjusted price. After every quote is handled and restatement is finalized.
Best fit A large book or a short maintenance window. A book small enough to finish while trading stays paused.
Preparation Muon must be ready to publish the adjusted price. Inventory and PartyB funding state must be ready before the effective-time freeze.
Quote rewrite Optional. It can happen later in a separate maintenance window. Required before this maintenance window can end.

What both routes share

Both routes begin with the same scheduled transition:

  1. Operations verifies the external event and calls scheduleAdjustment(symbolId, factor, effectiveTimestamp).
  2. Trading continues in the old units until the effective timestamp.
  3. At that timestamp, Core freezes price-sensitive actions for the symbol. No keeper or separate freeze transaction is required.
  4. Operations checks whether the venue changed units as announced, then chooses a route.

The factor is the number of new units received for one old unit, scaled by 1e18. A 4:1 split uses 4e18. A 1:10 reverse split uses 0.1e18. Core accepts factors from 0.01e18 to 100e18, excluding 1e18.

If the event is cancelled or postponed, operations can call cancelAdjustment, even after the effective time. Core then removes the scheduled freeze without activating the factor.

A past effective time freezes only after scheduling. An emergency schedule may name an effective time that has already passed. Core cannot have frozen the symbol before the transaction existed, so it records the scheduling block time separately. That time matters if operations goes straight into restatement.

Route 1: resume with adjusted prices

This route leaves every quote in its old units. Muon converts the venue's new raw price back into those units, and Core stores the conversion factor as active.

  1. Muon waits for the venue feed to change units and reads getProspectiveCumulativeFactor(symbolId).
  2. Muon calculates and checks the old-unit price.
  3. Operations independently verifies that price basis.
  4. Operations calls confirmPriceAdjusted(symbolId).
  5. Core moves the adjustment to PRICE_ADJUSTED, activates the cumulative factor, and unfreezes the symbol.
adjustedPrice =
    rawVenuePrice * getCumulativeFactor(symbolId) / 1e18

After confirmation, Muon can resume affected account-level signatures with the adjusted price. Solvers keep using the old on-chain quantity and price basis, but hedge the remaining exposure: 

venueOpenAmount =
    (quantity - closedAmount) * cumulativeFactor / 1e18

The active factor may remain in place indefinitely. Later adjustments compound into it. Operations can also fold it into stored quotes during a later restatement window.

Restating later

A later restatement is optional, but it has the same quote and funding rules as direct restatement. The difference is timing. Trading is live before this maintenance window, so PartyBs can prepare funding immediately before operations calls startRestatement.

Starting the window freezes the symbol again. Muon pauses affected account-level signatures until finalization. If operations aborts before the first mutation, Core returns to active-factor trading and Muon can resume with adjusted prices. Before any mutation, operations must rerun the full preflight against the now-frozen book.

Route 2: rewrite quotes while frozen

This route never activates the scheduled factor for trading. The symbol remains frozen while Core converts its stored quotes. After finalization, the quotes and Muon's raw venue price are both in the new units.

Direct restatement must be prepared before the effective time. Once the symbol freezes, Core blocks funding-rate setters and epoch-duration changes. If any affected PartyB still has a nonzero current accumulated-funding rate, its quotes cannot be restated and the missing preparation cannot be fixed while the freeze remains.

Prepare before the freeze

Operations must first build the complete open and pending quote inventory. Then, for every affected symbol and PartyB pair:

  • no preparation is needed when accumulated funding is inactive, shown by epochDuration == 0; or
  • the PartyB must roll its accumulated funding state forward, set both currentLongRate and currentShortRate to zero, and let operations read the values back.

This rule keeps a price-denominated funding rate from spanning two quantity units. The stored accumulated amount can remain. Core charges it in the old units when each quote is rewritten.

If this preparation is incomplete when the freeze begins, operations should not start direct restatement. It can confirm the adjusted-price route once Muon is ready, reopen trading, prepare funding, and open a later restatement window.

Run the direct route

  1. At the effective time, verify that Core has frozen the symbol and Muon has stopped affected signatures.
  2. Do not call confirmPriceAdjusted.
  3. Call startRestatement(symbolId). Core copies the prospective factor into restatementFactor without changing cumulativeFactor.
  4. Rerun the full preflight against the frozen book. Do not mutate inventory if any check fails.
  5. Resolve pending quotes and rewrite every eligible open quote.
  6. Reconcile the complete inventory and wait for old-basis signatures to expire.
  7. Call finalizeRestatement(symbolId). Core marks the adjustment APPLIED, clears the factor, increments basisVersion, and unfreezes the symbol.

Muon then resumes affected account-level signatures with the raw venue price. No adjusted-price trading period occurs.

Abort behaves differently on this route

Operations may call abortRestatement only before any quote rewrite or pending-quote removal. On the direct route, abort closes the restatement window but returns to the already-effective SCHEDULED state. The symbol stays frozen. Operations must then confirm the factor, cancel the adjustment, or start restatement again.

How restatement works

The same restatement engine serves the direct route and an optional later cleanup. Core handles one declared batch at a time. It does not enumerate the complete book.

Preflight the whole book

Before opening a restatement window, an indexer or operations service must identify every eligible open quote and every unresolved pending quote. Operations calls previewQuoteAdjustment for each open quote. The preview uses the same conversion arithmetic and underflow checks as the write path, but it does not check liquidation state or prove that funding settlement can succeed.

After the symbol is frozen, operations must perform one final full-manifest check before the first rewrite or pending-quote removal. Re-run previewQuoteAdjustment for every open quote in the frozen manifest, including any quote found or changed since the preliminary scan. Recheck quote eligibility, PartyA and PartyB liquidation state, the accumulated-funding condition, and whether the old-unit funding settlement can complete. For a later restatement, this check happens after startRestatement opens the freeze. If any quote fails, abort or switch routes before the first mutation.

Rewrite open quotes

A PartyB may call applyAdjustment for its own quotes. A caller with SYMBOL_MANAGER_ROLE may process any quote. Eligible quote states are OPENED, CLOSE_PENDING, and CANCEL_CLOSE_PENDING.

For each quote, Core performs this sequence atomically:

  1. Check the PartyB's accumulated-funding state.
  2. Charge the quote's remaining accumulated funding in the old units.
  3. Remove the quote from position and funding aggregates.
  4. Convert every stored quantity and matching price field.
  5. Re-add the quote to the aggregates in the new units.
  6. Stamp the quote with the current restatement epoch.
newQuantity = oldQuantity * factor / 1e18
newPrice    = oldQuantity * oldPrice / newQuantity

The conversion covers the main quantity, closed amount, and requested close amount, together with their price fields. It also converts the initial, requested-open, and market prices. Collateral locks, fee configuration, and fees already charged do not change.

The quote epoch prevents a second rewrite in the same window. A later window may rewrite the quote again under a new epoch.

Remove pending quotes

Pending quotes do not have positions to convert. Operations can call cancelPendingQuotes for PENDING, LOCKED, and CANCEL_PENDING quotes while the symbol is frozen. The function follows normal expiry accounting, including fee refunds and lock release.

Read a mixed book

A multi-transaction restatement temporarily contains both old-unit and new-unit quotes. getQuoteInVenueUnits and getQuotesInVenueUnits return normalized copies for valuation, display, and hedging. They leave quotes already processed in the current epoch unchanged and virtually convert the rest. Core execution still expects the raw values from getQuote.

Finish only after reconciliation

The first successful rewrite or pending-quote removal sets restatementMutated. From that point, abort is blocked because reopening would expose a mixed book.

Core does not prove that the book is complete. Finalization checks authorization, window state, and signature expiry. Operations must prove that every eligible quote was rewritten or removed before calling finalizeRestatement.

Safety boundaries

The freeze blocks price-sensitive execution

A symbol is frozen after an effective scheduled adjustment and during every restatement window.

Blocked while frozen Reason
Quote creation, open and close fills, batch wrappers, and solver-fee wrappers They create or settle exposure at a symbol price.
Emergency close, ADL close, force-close price steps, liquidation price storage, and UPNL settlement They store or consume prices in a specific unit basis.
Funding charges, funding-rate setters, and epoch-duration changes Funding values are denominated per unit.

Non-price cleanup may remain available. Users and solvers can still cancel, unlock, or expire eligible requests through normal paths. Operations can use cancelPendingQuotes during the freeze.

Old signatures must expire before the basis changes

Muon signatures do not include basisVersion. This keeps their payloads compatible with v0.8.5, but it means a signature created before the freeze may remain valid while quotes are being rewritten. Core therefore keeps the symbol frozen until the current maximum validity window has passed. 

require(
    block.timestamp > adjustment.restatementStartedAt + LibMuon.maxUpnlValidTime(),
    "SymbolAdjustmentFacet: Restatement window too short"
);

maxUpnlValidTime() uses the longest value among the global UPNL validity time and every per-function override. Finalization at the exact expiry boundary still reverts. This check uses the validity configuration in force when finalizeRestatement executes.

The start of that waiting period depends on how restatement began:

  • For direct restatement, it is the later of effectiveTimestamp and the block time recorded by scheduleAdjustment.
  • For a later restatement, it is the block time when startRestatement opens the new freeze.

This distinction prevents a past-effective emergency schedule from backdating the safety window. The recorded freeze start therefore matches what happened on-chain.

Do not make an old signature valid again. Muon validity settings remain mutable. Increasing the global or a per-function window after finalization can make an old timestamp valid again because signatures do not carry the symbol's basis version. Operations must retain the freeze-start time, compute the maximum validity under the proposed configuration, and delay the increase until the current block time is strictly greater than the freeze start plus that maximum.

Account-level signatures need an off-chain pause

Core can block a call when the call names the symbol or a known quote. An account-level UPNL signature can cover several symbols without listing them, so Core cannot identify every affected account from the payload. Muon must stop issuing those signatures while an exposed symbol is frozen.

Rounding cannot erase live amounts

The preview and write paths reject a converted total quantity of zero, a nonzero closed amount that becomes zero, a remaining open amount of zero, and a nonzero close request that becomes zero. For valid conversions, integer rounding may leave less than one wei of price per converted unit as notional dust.

Deferred force closes cannot cross a finalized restatement

Finalization increments basisVersion. A three-step force close records the version when it starts and rejects finalization if the symbol's version changed. The caller must initialize that force close again against the new basis.

Integration responsibilities

Actor Required behavior
Operations Verify the external event, schedule it, choose a prepared route, reconcile the full quote inventory, enforce the signature wait, and decide when finalization is safe.
Muon Read the active, prospective, and freeze state. Stop account-level signatures for exposed accounts during a freeze. Resume with adjusted prices after factor confirmation or raw prices after restatement finalization.
PartyBs Prepare accumulated funding before the relevant freeze, hedge in the active basis, and call applyAdjustment only for their own quotes unless they also hold the manager role.
Indexers Reconstruct history from events, build the full open and pending inventory, and reconcile every quote against the current restatement epoch.
Frontends Show the scheduled event and maintenance state. Use normalized venue-unit views during an active factor or a mixed restatement window. Do not pass normalized copies back into Core execution.

Operations runbook

Before scheduling

  1. Verify the symbol, factor direction, effective time, and venue notice.
  2. Build an initial open and pending inventory.
  3. If direct restatement may be used, have every affected PartyB prepare its funding pair and verify the resulting values before the effective time.
  4. Schedule the adjustment and record the emitted adjustment index and block time.

At the effective time

  1. Verify that the venue feed changed units and Core reports the symbol as frozen.
  2. Verify that Muon stopped affected account-level signatures.
  3. Cancel the adjustment if the event did not happen.
  4. Otherwise, confirm the adjusted price to resume quickly, or remain frozen and continue with direct restatement only when its inventory and funding preparation are complete.

During any restatement

  1. Preview every open quote before opening the window.
  2. Start the window and record its epoch, factor, and start time.
  3. While the symbol is frozen and before any mutation, re-scan the complete manifest. Re-run previewQuoteAdjustment for every open quote, then recheck quote eligibility, liquidation state, funding rates, and old-unit funding settlement. Abort or switch routes if any item fails.
  4. Only after that final check, remove pending quotes and process open quotes in retryable batches.
  5. Verify that every processed quote reports the current epoch.
  6. Re-scan the book and reconcile the complete manifest.
  7. Wait until block.timestamp > restatementStartedAt + maxUpnlValidTime().
  8. Finalize, verify the factor returned to 1e18, then resume funding and Muon in raw venue units.

Contract reference

Lifecycle functions

Function Purpose
scheduleAdjustment Record a factor and effective time.
cancelAdjustment Cancel a scheduled step when no restatement window is open.
confirmPriceAdjusted Activate the prospective factor and unfreeze the symbol.
startRestatement Open a frozen window from an effective schedule or an active factor.
abortRestatement Close a window that has not changed any inventory.
applyAdjustment Rewrite eligible open quotes.
cancelPendingQuotes Force-expire eligible pending quotes while frozen.
finalizeRestatement Close the window, reset factors, advance the basis version, and unfreeze.

All lifecycle functions except applyAdjustment require SYMBOL_MANAGER_ROLE. A quote's PartyB may apply the adjustment to its own quote. Views and previews are public.

Primary views

  • getSymbolAdjustment returns the raw per-symbol lifecycle record.
  • getCumulativeFactor returns the active factor and normalizes an unset zero to 1e18.
  • getProspectiveCumulativeFactor includes the latest scheduled factor only while state is SCHEDULED; otherwise it returns the active factor.
  • isSymbolFrozen is the execution freeze indicator.
  • getRestatementState returns the open-window flag and epoch.
  • getQuoteRestatedEpoch reports a quote's last processed window.
  • previewQuoteAdjustment returns the exact prospective stored values.

Lifecycle states

State Meaning
NONE No adjustment has been scheduled for the symbol.
SCHEDULED The factor is registered. The symbol freezes when the effective time arrives.
PRICE_ADJUSTED The adjusted Muon price is confirmed and the cumulative factor is active.
APPLIED The manager finalized restatement and Core reset the active factor. Complete quote coverage remains an operational claim.
CANCELLED The latest scheduled step was cancelled without activating its factor.

The state alone does not show whether a restatement window is open. Read it with restating, restatementFactor, and isSymbolFrozen.

Worked example: 4:1 split

An open quote starts in the old units:

quantity      = 1
openedPrice   = 400
notional      = 400

After the venue split, its raw price is 100.

If trading resumes first

raw venue price = 100
active factor   = 4
adjusted price  = 100 * 4 = 400

The quote and Muon price remain in old units, so UPNL stays continuous. Core can keep this factor active until a later restatement.

If the quote is restated

new quantity    = 1 * 4 = 4
new openedPrice = 1 * 400 / 4 = 100
new notional    = 4 * 100 = 400

The notional remains 400. After finalization, Core resets the active factor to 1e18 and Muon uses the raw venue price of 100.

If operations prepared the full inventory and every PartyB funding pair before the effective-time freeze, it can make this rewrite through direct restatement. Otherwise it resumes on the adjusted price first and performs the same rewrite later.
