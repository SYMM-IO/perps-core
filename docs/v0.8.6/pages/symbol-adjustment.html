Symbol Corporate-Action Adjustment

A safe way to move a symbol through a stock split, reverse split, or rebase without creating false PNL from a change of units.

Summary

A corporate action can change both the price unit and the quantity unit of a market while leaving its economic value unchanged. In a 4:1 split, one old unit becomes four new units and the venue price becomes roughly one quarter of its previous value.

An open Symmio quote still contains its old quantity and old opened price. If an oracle compares that quote with the venue's new raw price, the units no longer match and the resulting UPNL is wrong. The Symbol Adjustment feature prevents that mismatch with one mandatory freeze and two operational routes:

  1. Required transition freeze. Operations schedules the change and Core freezes the symbol at the effective time before any new-basis price can be used.
  2. Active-factor route. For a large book or when trading must resume quickly, Muon moves to the adjusted price basis, operations confirms the factor, and trading resumes before optional later restatement.
  3. Direct-restatement route. For a small book, operations keeps the symbol frozen and starts restatement directly from the effective scheduled adjustment. Quotes move to venue units before trading resumes, so the scheduled factor never becomes an active Muon or trading factor.
Confirmation is optional when restatement is immediate. confirmPriceAdjusted activates a factor and unfreezes without rewriting positions. startRestatement may instead consume an effective scheduled factor while remaining frozen; finalization then resumes trading directly in raw venue units.

Scope and Terms

Term Meaning
Factor New units received for one old unit, scaled by 1e18. A 4:1 split is 4e18; a 1:10 reverse split is 0.1e18.
Active factor The product of confirmed adjustments that have not yet been folded into quotes.
Prospective factor The active factor multiplied by the currently scheduled factor. It can be confirmed for trading or selected for direct restatement.
Restatement factor The factor fixed for one frozen restatement window. Direct mode stores it separately without changing the active factor.
Restatement Rewriting stored quote quantities and per-unit prices so the quote itself carries the new unit basis.

This feature is intended for unit-changing events such as stock splits, reverse splits, and rebases. It does not model cash dividends, spin-offs, mergers, ticker migrations, or ordinary FX price movement. Those events require different accounting or migration flows.

Each scheduled factor must be between 0.01e18 and 100e18. A factor of exactly 1e18 is rejected because it changes no units. Confirmed factors compound with full-precision arithmetic, and a product that rounds down to zero is rejected.

Who Does What

The protocol process crosses several systems. Core never calls Muon, and the venue never calls Core.

Actor Responsibility Interaction with Core
Venue or issuer Announces the event and changes the external market's quantity and price units. None. This is an external notice and market change.
Operations Verifies the event, schedules the factor, then chooses active-factor trading or direct frozen restatement based on inventory size and operational needs. Calls manager-gated Symbol Adjustment functions.
Symmio Core Stores per-symbol state, computes the freeze, gates unsafe paths, and rewrites quotes during restatement. Exposes state and enforces rules when another actor calls it.
Muon Reads Core state and venue prices off-chain, then produces prices and UPNL signatures in the correct unit basis. Reads the registry. Core does not send a transaction to Muon.
Solvers Quote and hedge using the active factor. During restatement, each PartyB may rewrite its own quotes. Reads factor state and may call applyAdjustment.
Indexers and frontends Track the lifecycle, display maintenance state, convert live values, and build the quote inventory for restatement. Read views and events.

Lifecycle

After the effective-time freeze, operations chooses whether to reopen on an active factor or finish restatement before reopening. 

flowchart TD
	A["Venue announces a unit change"] --> B["Operations schedules factor and effective time"]
	B --> C{"Effective time reached"}
	C -->|No| D["Trading continues in old units"]
	D --> C
	C -->|Yes| E["Core freezes the symbol automatically"]
	E --> F{"Operations chooses route"}
	F -->|Event cancelled or postponed| G["Cancel adjustment and unfreeze"]
	F -->|Large book or trading must resume| H["Muon verifies prospective basis"]
	H --> I["Confirm factor and unfreeze"]
	I --> J["Trade on active factor"]
	J -->|Optional later cleanup| K["Open frozen restatement window"]
	F -->|Small book| L["Start direct restatement while still frozen"]
	K --> M["Restate all declared quotes"]
	L --> M
	M --> N["Finalize, clear factors, and resume raw venue price"]

1. Schedule

Operations calls scheduleAdjustment(symbolId, factor, effectiveTimestamp). Core records the latest step and increments scheduledCount. Trading continues normally until the effective timestamp. Only one scheduled step may be in flight per symbol.

2. Transition freeze

At the effective timestamp, isSymbolFrozen becomes true automatically. There is no freeze transaction and no keeper race. The scheduled factor is not active yet. Muon reads getProspectiveCumulativeFactor, waits for the source feed to change units, and calculates prices in the old on-chain basis. It must withhold affected account-level signatures during this handoff.

3. Choose the route

If the venue cancels or postpones the event, operations calls cancelAdjustment. The symbol unfreezes and the scheduled factor is never used. Otherwise operations chooses between confirmation and direct restatement. Direct restatement is available only after the effective timestamp and keeps the symbol frozen for the complete rewrite.

4A. Confirm and trade on the active factor

Operations first verifies Muon's prospective price basis, then calls confirmPriceAdjusted. Core compounds the factor, moves the latest state to PRICE_ADJUSTED, and unfreezes the symbol.

Open quotes still use their old quantity and price units. Muon publishes an adjusted price in the same old basis, solvers hedge the corresponding number of venue units, and frontends convert values for display. This state may remain active indefinitely. Additional confirmed adjustments compound into the same cumulative factor.

4B. Restate directly while frozen

If the open and pending inventory is small, operations may call startRestatement directly from the effective SCHEDULED state. Core fixes the prospective factor into restatementFactor, leaves cumulativeFactor unchanged, and keeps the symbol frozen. After every declared quote is handled, finalization marks the adjustment APPLIED, clears both factor paths, and resumes trading in raw venue units.

5. Restate later when desired

The active-factor route may remain live indefinitely or later open a second freeze and perform the same restatement process.

Freeze Boundary

A symbol is frozen when either condition is true:

  • the latest step is SCHEDULED and its effective timestamp has been reached; or
  • a restatement window is open.

The same library guard is applied across price-sensitive execution:

Blocked while frozen Why
New quotes, open fills, close fills, batch wrappers, and solver-fee wrappers They would create or settle exposure against an uncertain price basis.
Emergency close, ADL close, and force-close price steps They store or consume symbol prices.
Classic and accumulated funding charges, rate setters, and epoch-duration changes Funding rates are per-unit values and must not cross unit systems.
Liquidation price storage, UPNL settlement, and Clearing House close paths These paths depend on symbol-specific prices.

Non-price state management can remain available. Users and solvers may cancel, unlock, or expire pending requests through normal paths. Operations may also call cancelPendingQuotes in either frozen window. A liquidation that stored compatible prices before the freeze may continue through later non-price steps.

Price Basis

While quotes remain in old units, Muon calculates the active price as:

activePrice = rawVenuePrice * getCumulativeFactor(symbolId) / 1e18

During the transition freeze, getCumulativeFactor intentionally returns only confirmed factors. Muon reads the factor that would become active after confirmation from: 

prospectiveFactor = getProspectiveCumulativeFactor(symbolId)
prospectivePrice  = rawVenuePrice * prospectiveFactor / 1e18
A factor is not a source-feed signal. The prospective getter provides arithmetic only. Muon must independently verify that the venue source has changed to the new unit basis before using it, and operations must verify Muon's output before confirming. Direct restatement does not ask Muon to activate the prospective factor because the symbol remains frozen until quotes are stored in the venue basis.

While a factor is active, solvers hedge onChainQuantity * factor / 1e18 venue units. Frontends use the same quantity conversion and display onChainPrice * 1e18 / factor as the venue-native price. During restatement, the book temporarily contains mixed units, so consumers must show maintenance state instead of applying one global display conversion.

Normalized quote reads

getQuoteInVenueUnits and getQuotesInVenueUnits return quote copies normalized to the selected venue basis. Outside restatement they use the confirmed active factor. During either restatement route, they leave quotes already processed in the current epoch unchanged and virtually convert remaining quotes with restatementFactor. The batch getter evaluates every quote in one on-chain snapshot, allowing operators, solvers, and frontends to inspect a consistent mixed book even when cumulativeFactor was never activated.

Each result includes the applied factor, restatement epoch, stored-unit status, and symbol freeze status. These normalized quotes are for valuation, display, and external hedging. Core execution calls still require the raw stored values returned by getQuote.

Restatement

Restatement folds the factor selected for the window into stored quotes. It may start directly from an effective scheduled adjustment or later from an active confirmed factor. It is optional only when active-factor trading is acceptable; once a window has processed any quote, that window must be completed.

Preflight and manifest

Core cannot enumerate the complete quote inventory. An indexer or operations service must build a manifest containing every eligible open quote and every unresolved pending quote. Operations previews every open quote, then calls: 

startRestatement(symbolId)

This opens a new epoch, stores the selected restatementFactor, resets restatementMutated, and freezes the symbol. From SCHEDULED, the selected value is the prospective factor and the active cumulativeFactor is not changed. From a confirmed state, the selected value is the active factor. Core does not accept or trust off-chain inventory counts. The symbol manager remains responsible for reconciling the complete manifest before finalization.

Open quotes

A PartyB may call applyAdjustment for its own quotes. A symbol manager may process any quote. Eligible states are OPENED, CLOSE_PENDING, and CANCEL_CLOSE_PENDING. Every batch is atomic. 

1. Require zero current accumulated-funding rates for the symbol and PartyB.
2. Charge all accumulated funding still denominated in the old unit.
3. Remove the quote from aggregated position and funding state.
4. Convert every stored quantity and per-unit price field.
5. Re-add the quote to aggregate state in the new unit.
6. Stamp the quote with the current epoch and mark the window as mutated.

The conversion covers all unit-sensitive quote fields:

Quantity fields Matching price fields
quantity openedPrice, initialOpenedPrice, requestedOpenPrice, marketPrice
closedAmount avgClosedPrice
quantityToClose requestedClosePrice

Prices are calculated from the old notional and the converted quantity. Clean factors preserve notional exactly. Other factors may round down by less than one wei of price per converted unit. Collateral locks, fee configuration, and already charged fee totals are not rescaled.

Pending quotes

Pending quotes have no open position to restate. Operations may force-expire PENDING, LOCKED, and CANCEL_PENDING quotes with cancelPendingQuotes. Normal user cancellation, PartyB cancellation, force cancellation, manager expiry, and pending liquidation cleanup also mark the window as mutated when they remove a quote from PartyA's pending inventory.

Abort or finalize

abortRestatement is available only while restatementMutated is false. It closes an unused window and clears restatementFactor. A confirmed-factor window returns to active-factor trading. A direct window returns to the effective SCHEDULED state, so the transition freeze remains until operations confirms, cancels, or retries. A successful quote rewrite or pending-inventory removal sets the flag and disables abort, preventing a partially converted book from reopening.

finalizeRestatement is a symbol-manager decision. Core does not claim that every quote was processed. Finalization resets cumulativeFactor to 1e18, clears restatementFactor, closes the window, and unfreezes the symbol. Both SCHEDULED direct restatement and PRICE_ADJUSTED later restatement move the latest state to APPLIED.

Safety Boundaries

Funding must cross the unit boundary once

Accumulated funding rates are price-denominated per unit. Before restatement, the current long and short rates for every affected PartyB must be zero. applyAdjustment then charges the remaining old-unit funding before conversion. Rate setters and epoch-duration changes stay frozen until finalization. New rates are set afterward using the raw post-restatement price.

Unsafe rounding is rejected before mutation

previewQuoteAdjustment uses the exact write-path arithmetic. It rejects output overflow, a total quantity that becomes zero, a nonzero closed amount that becomes zero, a remaining open amount that becomes zero, or a nonzero close request that becomes zero. Operations must resolve such quotes before opening a restatement window.

Account-level signatures remain an off-chain boundary

Core can freeze paths that receive a symbol ID or operate on a known quote. It cannot determine which symbols are represented inside an account-level UPNL signature. Muon must therefore withhold fresh account-level signatures for every account exposed to a frozen symbol. A still-valid signature issued before the freeze is not invalidated on-chain.

Finalization is a trusted operational boundary. Core verifies per-quote idempotency and prevents abort after a partial mutation, but it cannot prove complete quote coverage. Before finalizing, operations must independently verify every eligible quote, the venue source-unit change, and the factor selected for the window. On the active-factor route, this also requires verifying Muon's price basis.

Storage layout and scope

Adjustment state is isolated per symbol. The adjustments mapping stores one SymbolAdjustment record for each symbolId; it does not store a history of earlier scheduled steps. Indexers reconstruct that history from events. The second mapping stores the last restatement window that physically rewrote each quote. 

struct Layout {
    mapping(uint256 symbolId => SymbolAdjustment) adjustments;
    mapping(uint256 quoteId => uint256 epoch) quoteRestatedEpoch;
}
Read raw storage and effective state differently. getSymbolAdjustment returns the stored struct exactly. Before any factor is activated, its raw cumulativeFactor is 0. Operational integrations should normally use getCumulativeFactor, which translates raw 0 to the effective identity factor 1e18.

AdjustmentState

The enum describes the latest scheduled adjustment step. It does not by itself describe whether a restatement window is open. Always interpret it together with restating, cumulativeFactor, and isSymbolFrozen.

Value Meaning Factor behavior Freeze behavior Normal next action
NONE (0) No adjustment has ever been scheduled for this symbol. Effective factor is 1e18; raw storage may still be 0. Not frozen by adjustment state. scheduleAdjustment
SCHEDULED (1) The latest factor and effective time are registered but not confirmed. The scheduled factor appears in getProspectiveCumulativeFactor and may be copied into restatementFactor. Unfrozen before the effective time; frozen at and after it. After effective time: confirmPriceAdjusted, startRestatement, or cancelAdjustment.
PRICE_ADJUSTED (2) Operations confirmed that Muon is using the adjusted price basis. The latest factor is compounded into cumulativeFactor. Not frozen by the enum; trading is active unless restating is true. Keep trading, schedule another step, or optionally start restatement.
APPLIED (3) The symbol manager finalized after operational quote-inventory reconciliation. cumulativeFactor was reset to 1e18. Unfrozen after finalization. Trade in the raw basis or schedule a later adjustment.
CANCELLED (4) The latest scheduled step was cancelled without activating its own factor. An older confirmed cumulativeFactor may still remain active. Not frozen by the cancelled step. Schedule another step, or restate an older active factor if one remains.

SymbolAdjustment fields

Field Type and units Written or reset by Operational interpretation
factor uint256, 1e18-scaled units multiplier Overwritten by every successful scheduleAdjustment. Describes only the latest scheduled step: a 4:1 split is 4e18; a 1:10 reverse split is 0.1e18. Allowed range is 0.01e18 through 100e18, excluding 1e18. Cancellation and finalization do not clear this field, so it must not be read as the active factor.
effectiveTimestamp uint256, Unix timestamp in seconds Overwritten by scheduleAdjustment. While state is SCHEDULED, the symbol freezes when block.timestamp >= effectiveTimestamp. A past timestamp is valid and freezes immediately. The timestamp remains stored after confirm, cancel, or finalize but no longer controls freezing unless state is SCHEDULED.
state AdjustmentState, numeric values 0 through 4 Set by schedule, cancel, confirm, and finalization of either restatement route. Describes the latest scheduled step, not the entire symbol condition. startRestatement does not change it; the separate restating flag supplies that overlay.
scheduledCount uint256, monotonically increasing count Incremented once by every successful scheduleAdjustment; never reset. When the count is nonzero, the latest adjustment event index is scheduledCount - 1. Cancellation and confirmation do not increment it.
cumulativeFactor uint256, 1e18-scaled cumulative multiplier Compounded by confirmPriceAdjusted; reset to 1e18 by finalizeRestatement. Product of all confirmed factors not yet folded into quote storage. Raw 0 means uninitialized and is treated as 1e18 by getCumulativeFactor. Compounding uses Math.mulDiv with downward integer rounding and rejects a result of zero. It can remain active indefinitely without restatement. Direct restatement does not activate the scheduled factor here.
restatementEpoch uint256, monotonically increasing window ID Incremented at the start of every startRestatement, including a window later aborted. Identifies a restatement attempt. It is not a factor, timestamp, or permanent quote-unit version, and it is never decremented.
restating bool Set to true by startRestatement; set to false by a permitted abort or successful finalization. When true, the symbol is frozen regardless of state. Finalization remains an explicit SYMBOL_MANAGER_ROLE decision.
restatementMutated bool Reset to false by startRestatement; set true by a quote rewrite or pending-inventory removal. Authoritative abort-safety flag. Once true, abortRestatement must revert because the book may contain mixed stored units. It is not a progress counter and does not prove complete quote coverage.
restatementFactor uint256, 1e18-scaled window multiplier Set by startRestatement; cleared to 0 by abort or finalization. Factor used by applyAdjustment, previews, and normalized mixed-book views. From SCHEDULED it equals the prospective factor, allowing direct restatement without changing Muon's active cumulativeFactor. A raw 0 means no window is open.

Mapping fields

Mapping Key and default Meaning and limitations
adjustments[symbolId] One record per symbol; an unknown or untouched key reads as an all-zero struct. Stores only the latest scheduled step plus the active cumulative and last restatement bookkeeping. Use the event stream for full history.
quoteRestatedEpoch[quoteId] One value per quote; default 0 means never physically restated. Set to the symbol's current restatementEpoch after a successful rewrite. It prevents the same quote from being processed twice in one window and lets normalized views distinguish processed from unprocessed quotes during that window. It does not store the factor and is not a permanent proof of the quote's current basis.

Reading a restatement snapshot

state                       = PRICE_ADJUSTED
restating                   = true
restatementEpoch            = 7
restatementMutated          = true
cumulativeFactor            = 4e18
restatementFactor           = 4e18
quoteRestatedEpoch[410]     = 7
quoteRestatedEpoch[411]     = 6

This means the symbol is frozen in restatement window 7 and at least one on-chain mutation has occurred. Quote 410 was physically converted in this window; quote 411 was not. Abort must revert because restatementMutated is true. Core does not know how many additional quotes remain, so the symbol manager must reconcile the off-chain inventory before finalizing and resetting the active 4e18 factor.

In a direct window, state remains SCHEDULED, cumulativeFactor may remain raw 0 (effective identity), and restatementFactor carries 4e18. That distinction proves the scheduled factor was selected for maintenance but never activated for trading.

Integration Requirements

Muon

  • Read the latest adjustment, active factor, prospective factor, and freeze state for every supported symbol.
  • Use the prospective factor for signatures only when operations chooses the active-factor route and the source feed has changed units.
  • Withhold symbol-specific and account-level signatures during both frozen windows.
  • Resume after confirmation only when the active factor matches the verified price basis.
  • For direct restatement, do not activate the scheduled factor; resume after finalization when quotes and funding use raw venue units.
  • Detect an unscheduled price discontinuity and alert operations before signing it.

Solvers

  • Prepare for the effective timestamp when AdjustmentScheduled is emitted.
  • While a factor is active, hedge and submit prices in the old on-chain basis.
  • Before restatement, roll accumulated funding and set the current long and short rates to zero.
  • During restatement, process owned quotes and reconcile every emitted epoch.
  • After finalization, restart accumulated funding using the raw post-restatement price.

Frontends

  • Show the scheduled event and effective time before the freeze.
  • Distinguish active-factor trading, direct restatement during the transition freeze, and optional later restatement.
  • Use the active factor for venue-native display while positions remain in old units.
  • Do not apply one global conversion during restatement, when quote units are mixed.

Indexers and monitoring

  • Reconstruct adjustment history from events and keep current state per symbol.
  • Build the complete open and pending quote manifest before restatement.
  • Reconcile every eligible quote ID against the current epoch before authorizing finalization.
  • Alert on an effective scheduled step that remains unconfirmed, an aborted window, a stalled batch, or a factor that does not reset.

Operations Runbook

Common transition and route choice

  1. Verify the symbol ID, factor direction, effective time, and source-feed change.
  2. Read the current and prospective factors and sanity-check representative prices.
  3. Call scheduleAdjustment and record the adjustment index.
  4. At the effective time, confirm the symbol is frozen and stop fresh signatures for exposed accounts.
  5. Call cancelAdjustment if the event did not occur. Otherwise choose direct restatement when the inventory is small enough to finish while frozen, or active-factor confirmation when trading must resume first.

Active-factor route

  1. Have Muon calculate on the prospective basis and independently compare it with the raw source.
  2. Call confirmPriceAdjusted after the new basis is verified.
  3. Verify the emitted cumulative factor and the unfreeze before reopening integrations.

Direct or later restatement

  1. Snapshot the complete open and pending quote inventory from the indexer.
  2. Preview every open quote and resolve rounding, overflow, liquidation, and funding-settlement failures.
  3. Roll accumulated funding, set current long and short rates to zero, and read them back.
  4. Call startRestatement(symbolId) and verify the emitted epoch and factor. For direct mode, also verify state remains SCHEDULED and getCumulativeFactor did not activate the new factor.
  5. Resolve pending quotes and process open quotes in retryable batches.
  6. For every open quote, verify getQuoteRestatedEpoch(quoteId) equals the current epoch.
  7. Re-scan the open and pending inventory and verify no old-basis quote was omitted.
  8. Finalize, confirm the factor is 1e18, restart funding in the raw price basis, then resume Muon signatures.
Abort before the first mutation. If the operation is cancelled while restatementMutated is false, abort the window and retry later. After the first successful rewrite or pending removal, finish the inventory reconciliation and finalize instead. Aborting direct mode returns to the effective scheduled freeze; it does not reopen trading.

Worked Example: 4:1 Split

An open quote starts with:

quantity      = 1
openedPrice   = 400
notional      = 400

The venue performs a 4:1 split and reopens near 100. Using that raw price against the old quote would create a false price move from 400 to 100. During the active-factor stage, the quote remains unchanged and Muon reports: 

raw venue price = 100
active factor   = 4
adjusted price  = 100 * 4 = 400

UPNL remains continuous because the quote and oracle price are both in old units. If operations later restates the quote, Core writes: 

new quantity    = 1 * 4 = 4
new openedPrice = 1 * 400 / 4 = 100
new notional    = 4 * 100 = 400

The economic value never changes. Only the accounting unit changes. After finalization, the factor returns to 1e18 and Muon can use the venue's raw 100 price directly.

With only a few quotes, operations can skip the active-factor stage: keep the symbol frozen, start direct restatement with restatementFactor = 4e18, write the same 4/100 quote values, and finalize. Muon never needs to publish the temporary 400 adjusted price because no trading or price-sensitive execution occurs during the window.
