Symmio Core v0.8.6 Changelog

v0.8.6 is mostly about removing operational weak spots: withdrawals were safe but slow, AccountLayer ownership flows were too rigid for delegated operations, and PartyA liquidation settlement could depend on funding state that changed later. The release adds Express Withdrawal, delegated ownership controls, shutdown and freshness controls, and snapshot-backed liquidation settlement.

Changelog

New features

01 Express Withdrawal Express Withdrawal System Design SYMMIO's 12-hour cooldown protects withdrawals but slows exits. ExpressProvider fronts pool and credit-backed funds, then reconciles when the normal withdrawal finalizes. Open change 02 AccountLayer AccountLayer Delegated Creation & Ownership Transfer Account creation and ownership handoff were tied too closely to the caller-owner model. v0.8.6 adds role-gated creation for a target owner and direct owner-initiated SubAccount transfer. Open change 03 Liquidation PartyA Liquidation Snapshot Flow Funding at the liquidation timestamp could become unreconstructable after funding state moved. Snapshot liquidation stores the signed PartyB-symbol funding state before positions are closed. Open change 04 Liquidation Single-Step PartyA Liquidation After the v0.8.5 AccountLayer changes, many accounts carry one market or one position. This wrapper completes those small snapshot liquidations in one all-or-revert transaction. Open change 05 Clearing House Affiliate Shutdown Flow Deregistration stopped new affiliate relationships, but it did not give existing positions a clean exit path. v0.8.6 adds scheduled close-only shutdown, a solver window, and a Clearing House backstop. Open change 06 Muon Muon UPNL Validity Overrides One global UPNL freshness window forced every Muon flow to share the same tolerance. v0.8.6 adds optional per-function overrides that fall back to the global value when unset. Open change 07 Fees Solver Fees v0.8.6 adds explicit solver rate fees, charged from PartyA allocated balance and bounded by user-approved notional-rate caps plus execution-time solvency checks. Open change 08 Fees Operational Fees A unified per-op fee primitive shared by solvers and registered relayers, backed by a standing per-(payer, charger) allowance with timelocked reductions and a priority multiplier. Open change 09 Symbols Symbol Corporate-Action Adjustment Venue stock splits and rebases change the price unit while on-chain positions keep the old one. v0.8.6 adds an adjustment registry that freezes the symbol through the transition and a value-neutral restatement that rebases open positions. Minor improvements

Liquidation PartyA Liquidation Fee Recipient Cleanup Records the liquidation starter as the unambiguous recipient for capped PartyA liquidation fees. Events BalanceChange Event Cleanup Emits the matching BalanceChange type for reimbursement, fee, deferred-balance, and LF movements. Funding Lazy Accumulated Funding Quote flows no longer roll the symbol/PartyB funding state; funding settles lazily from persisted state, which now changes -- and emits its final state -- only on solver configuration. PartyB Allocation Suspension Gates Suspended PartyBs can no longer allocate or transfer allocation between PartyA buckets. Liquidation Cross-PartyB Liquidation Reserve Enforcement Pending cross-PartyB liquidation settlement reserves now reduce shared quote, solvency, and liquidation availability.
