Symmio Core v0.8.6 Changelog

v0.8.6 is mostly about removing operational weak spots: withdrawals were safe but slow, AccountLayer ownership flows were too rigid for delegated operations, and PartyA liquidation settlement could depend on funding state that changed later. The release adds Express Withdrawal, delegated ownership controls, shutdown and freshness controls, and snapshot-backed liquidation settlement.

Changelog

New features

01 Express Withdrawal Express Withdrawal System Design SYMMIO's 12-hour cooldown protects withdrawals but slows exits. ExpressProvider fronts pool and credit-backed funds, then reconciles when the normal withdrawal finalizes. Open change 02 AccountLayer AccountLayer Delegated Creation & Ownership Transfer Account creation and ownership handoff were tied too closely to the caller-owner model. v0.8.6 adds role-gated creation for a target owner and direct owner-initiated SubAccount transfer. Open change 03 Liquidation PartyA Liquidation Snapshot Flow Funding at the liquidation timestamp could become unreconstructable after funding state moved. Snapshot liquidation stores the signed PartyB-symbol funding state before positions are closed. Open change 04 Liquidation Single-Step PartyA Liquidation After the v0.8.5 AccountLayer changes, many accounts carry one market or one position. This wrapper completes those small snapshot liquidations in one all-or-revert transaction. Open change 05 Clearing House Affiliate Shutdown Flow Deregistration stopped new affiliate relationships, but it did not give existing positions a clean exit path. v0.8.6 adds scheduled close-only shutdown, a solver window, and a Clearing House backstop. Open change 06 Muon Muon UPNL Validity Overrides One global UPNL freshness window forced every Muon flow to share the same tolerance. v0.8.6 adds optional per-function overrides that fall back to the global value when unset.
