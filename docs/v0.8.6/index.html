Changelog

New features

01 Express Withdrawal Express Withdrawal System Design SYMMIO's 12-hour cooldown protects withdrawals but slows exits. ExpressProvider fronts pool and credit-backed funds, then reconciles when the normal withdrawal finalizes. 02 AccountLayer AccountLayer Delegated Creation & Ownership Transfer Account creation and ownership handoff were tied too closely to the caller-owner model. v0.8.6 adds role-gated creation for a target owner and direct owner-initiated SubAccount transfer. 03 Liquidation PartyA Liquidation Snapshot Flow Funding at the liquidation timestamp could become unreconstructable after funding state moved. Snapshot liquidation stores the signed PartyB-symbol funding state before positions are closed. 04 Liquidation Single-Step PartyA Liquidation After the v0.8.5 AccountLayer changes, many accounts carry one market or one position. This wrapper completes those small snapshot liquidations in one all-or-revert transaction. 05 Clearing House Affiliate Shutdown Flow Deregistration stopped new affiliate relationships, but it did not give existing positions a clean exit path. v0.8.6 adds scheduled close-only shutdown, a solver window, and a Clearing House backstop. 06 Muon Muon UPNL Validity Overrides One global UPNL freshness window forced every Muon flow to share the same tolerance. v0.8.6 adds optional per-function overrides that fall back to the global value when unset. 07 Fees Solver Fees v0.8.6 adds explicit solver rate fees, charged from PartyA allocated balance and bounded by user-approved notional-rate caps plus execution-time solvency checks. 08 Fees Operational Fees A unified per-op fee primitive shared by solvers and registered relayers, backed by a standing per-(payer, charger) allowance with timelocked reductions and a priority multiplier. 09 Events & Indexing Allocated Balance Event Ledger Every PartyA and PartyB allocated-balance mutation now emits an exact storage-backed delta. Dedicated reimbursement and liquidation settlement events preserve lifecycle context without corrupting the allocated ledger. 09 Symbols Symbol Corporate-Action Adjustment Venue stock splits and rebases change the price unit while on-chain positions keep the old one. v0.8.6 adds an adjustment registry that freezes the symbol through the transition and a value-neutral restatement that rebases open positions. Open change 09 Performance InstantOpen Gas Optimization The atomic margin-quote-lock-open flow ran as four signed operations and sat close to the HyperEVM small-block limit. Two additive entrypoints collapse it to two operations and cut roughly 155k gas. Open change

Minor improvements

Liquidation PartyA Liquidation Fee Recipient Cleanup Records the liquidation starter as the unambiguous recipient for capped PartyA liquidation fees. Funding Lazy Accumulated Funding Quote flows no longer roll the symbol/PartyB funding state; funding settles lazily from persisted state, which now changes -- and emits its final state -- only on solver configuration. PartyB Allocation Suspension Gates Suspended PartyBs can no longer allocate or transfer allocation between PartyA buckets. Liquidation Cross-PartyB Liquidation Reserve Enforcement Pending cross-PartyB liquidation settlement reserves now reduce shared quote, solvency, and liquidation availability. AccountLayer Delegation Account Scope A delegation was enforced against the account owner, so it reached every SubAccount that owner held; it is now confined to the account family it was granted over. Accounts Strict Deallocation PartyA and opted-in PartyBs preserve raw liquidation collateral during deallocation, with public views for the maximum safe amount. The safe paths additionally retain a Muon-attested floor re-marked to live notional, closing profit-financed collateral stripping. Fees Market Open Fee on Execution Price Market open fees are charged on the price the position opened at, not the market price PartyA signed. A bound PartyA could previously sign a zero market price and open the position for no fee.
